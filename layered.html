The chain: multi-altitude acoustic sensors give a vertical baseline → 3D corridor (bearing + elevation + range + velocity). The corridor cues a narrow-FOV thermal/EO head onto the exact patch of sky, handing a precise track to the effector — faster cue, higher hit quality. Iron Dome stays below its ~4 km minimum (excluded). Workhorses are the interceptor UAV (parallel tubes) and the C-RAM gun; both need the time that elevation buys. Watch leakers appear when detection is late (ground) and the swarm is large or fast.

Ranges/channels modeled from fielded systems (Zvook, Fraunhofer, Talon SECTR, published Iron Beam/Iron Dome figures). Service times and Pk are first-order — this is a timing/saturation tool, not a Pk predictor.
