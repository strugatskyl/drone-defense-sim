Counter-FPV Layered Defense

Interactive simulations exploring how to defend a fixed asset against fiber-optic and RF FPV drones — not by reacting faster, but by hearing earlier. Each tool is built on validated first-order physics and is honest about its own limits. They optimize defense; they give no advantage to an attacker.

On honesty: these are timing and saturation tools, not probability-of-kill predictors. Detection ranges and service times are first-order estimates drawn from fielded systems (Zvook, Fraunhofer, Talon SECTR, published Iron Beam / Iron Dome figures). Where a model reaches its limit, it says so.
