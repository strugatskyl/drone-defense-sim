What this shows: the same threat, defeated or not depending on when you first hear it. Elevated (aerostat) acoustic sensors detect at longer range — less ground clutter and no acoustic shadow zone — which converts tight or failed intercepts into comfortable ones, and lets cheap kinetic effectors (gun, interceptor UAV) do the job. Iron Dome sits below its ~4 km minimum range and cannot engage close FPV at any detection range. Laser needs early detection: its 3–5 s dwell eats the whole budget at close range.

Detection ranges (ground ≈250 m, aerostat ≈700 m at loudness 1.0) are modeled from fielded acoustic systems (Zvook, Fraunhofer, Talon SECTR); tune with the loudness slider.
Run with Ground sensors, then switch to Aerostat — watch the failing/marginal kills become comfortable. Push drone speed up and drop loudness to see the chain break.
