Model: kill mechanism is terminal displacement, not fiber break (the payout bobbin is a tension limiter — wind can't snap the fiber, but the same decoupling lets the drone be shoved off course). Drone is displaced crossing the disturbed zone, then operator/AI recovers within the time-to-impact budget. Human reaction ≈0.45 s, AI ≈0.08 s.

Limit of this model: point-mass, position only. It does NOT model attitude upset (pitch/roll tumble) — likely the real disruption mechanism. So it is optimistic for the attacker: fast drones "recover" here that a real quadrotor might not. Answering the fast-drone case honestly needs 6-DOF rigid-body sim or a bench test.
Run Wall OFF first (baseline ~100% strike). Then ON. Then push approach speed up with a thin zone — watch fast drones punch through. Watch the collateral panel.
